# Product decisions

The server comments in `apps/server/src/auth/routes.ts` and `apps/server/src/auth/usernames.ts` cite this file.

Each row is a choice the 30 September review asked the owner to make. **Recommendation** is the reviewer's recommendation, not an owner sign-off. **In the code** is what the repository does as of the 10 October 2026 commits. Where those differ, the code is not a decision.

Source of the options: [30 September review, section 6](../reviews/2026-09-30-claude-review.md). Status notes from [the 7 October recheck](../progress/11-october-7-review-recheck.md) and the [10 October logs](../progress/README.md).

| ID | Decision | Recommendation (not a sign-off) | In the code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Absence and disconnection | Sit out after the first time-out, and sit out on disconnect after a 60 s grace. Publish it. | A timed-out player is sat out (reason `TIMEOUT`). Sit-out after a disconnect grace period was still optional on 7 October. |
| D2 | Showdown mucking | Auto-muck losing hands unless shown. | **Open.** House Rules promise auto-muck. The engine still tables every live hand (NEW-3). |
| D3 | Idle closure | 5 min idle, then a 30 s warning. | 5 min, then a 10 s warning. Restart drops a warning in progress and gives every table a fresh 5 min. See the root README, "Table lifecycle". |
| D4 | Time bank | 20 s base plus a 30–60 s bank. | Wired on 7 October night: new tables get the configured bank (30 s, +5 s per 10 hands, max 60 s). Recovery gives the player to act a fresh base time. |
| D5 | Chip unit | Say "chips", not dollars. | The UI uses one chip formatter and does not show "$". Real-money currency is not chosen. |
| D6 | Hosting topology | One origin, web and API behind a proxy. | The Cloudflare demo is one `workers.dev` origin: `poker-web` forwards `/api` and `/ws` to `poker-api`. That is an implementation of same-origin hosting, not a recorded owner choice of vendor. The Node deploy docs still describe a separate proxy. |
| D7 | Usernames | Case-insensitive unique, reserved words, a limited script. | Implemented. Case-insensitive unique index. `guest_` / `bot_` prefixes and staff words are refused. See `apps/server/src/auth/usernames.ts`. |
| D8 | Buy-in default | A dialog, default `min(100 BB, bankroll)`. | "Sit here" opens a buy-in dialog. On 7 October the lobby's "Open 6-seat table" still took 1,000 chips in one click, and the default could be the whole bankroll. |
| D9 | Guests | Keep for play chips, age-gated, with an upgrade path. Remove before real money. | **Removed (owner, 2026-10-18): everyone signs up or signs in.** `POST /api/auth/guest` is gone (404). The account-security migration revoked live guest sessions (`GUEST_RETIRED`), and the edge refuses a guest session at authenticate, refresh and both socket checks. Guest rows stay (hand history references them); the `guest_` username prefix stays reserved. |
| D10 | Legal documents and markets | Counsel. | Six draft legal pages exist, with placeholders. Sign-up records the accepted terms version; when `TERMS_VERSION` changes, an existing account accepts the new version before the web app lets it play (`POST /api/auth/terms/accept`; the API itself does not yet refuse play on older terms). There is no server check that real-money mode matches an approved terms version, because there is no real-money mode. Target countries are not decided. Malta is the intended base named in the reviews. |
| D11 | Bots | Retire from production. Keep legacy tables only so seated players can finish. | New tables reject bots. `ENABLE_LEGACY_BOTS` defaults off: a bot's turn times out. Legacy bot tables stay visible to players still seated. |
| D12 | Unrecoverable hands | Void and refund, dual approval, an audit record. | **Open.** `pausedAt`, `TableIncident`, and `PAUSED` exist in the schema. On 7 October no server code used them to pause a broken table and refund it (CR-004). |
| D13 | Second factor | Offer and recommend TOTP and passkeys from sign-up; never force them; require a second factor for withdrawals when the account has one. | **Owner, 2026-10-18; edge API implemented 2026-10-19.** TOTP (with 10 backup codes) and passkeys are optional. A TOTP account's password sign-in also needs a code; a passkey with user verification signs in alone. Money actions need a single-use `StepUpGrant`: a passkey, the password plus a TOTP or backup code, or (only without either) the password plus an emailed code. There is no self-service 2FA reset by email: a user who lost the authenticator and the backup codes signs in with a passkey or goes through manual support. |
| D14 | Session lifetime | "Keep me signed in" on by default: 14 days idle, 30 days absolute. Off: a browser-session cookie, 12 h idle, 24 h absolute. Sensitive actions use a 5-minute step-up, not short sessions. | **Owner ("stay signed in"), 2026-10-18; implemented.** `SESSION_IDLE_TTL=14d`. Switching account is sign out, then sign in (the browser remembers up to 5 usernames, never passwords or tokens); a sign-in revokes the session of any refresh cookie sent with it. |
| D15 | Security hold | Hold withdrawals and payout-destination changes for 24 h after a credential change. | **Implemented** (`SECURITY_HOLD_HOURS`, default 24): password reset or change, email change, turning TOTP off, a "This wasn't me" lockdown, and a new TOTP or passkey enrolled after a step-up without a second factor (the password alone must not replace the emailed-code money proof at once) set `User.securityHoldUntil`. Nothing pays out today (real money is off). |
| D16 | Real-money asset | Book EUR only. Crypto is a payment rail: the crypto provider (BVNK) converts at deposit and at payout, so limits, AML thresholds, tax and reports stay in one currency and no signing key exists here. Sandbox money is `TEST_EUR`, never EUR. | **Schema implemented 2026-10-29, off.** Wallet accounts book `EUR` (live) or `TEST_EUR` (sandbox); the database keeps the sandbox provider on `TEST_EUR` and real providers off it. BTC, ETH, USDT and USDC rows stay disabled reference data for pay and payout currencies; USDT is never offered to EEA residents. `EUR` is disabled. |
| D17 | KYC timing | Identity verified before the first real-money deposit. The FIAU floor (CDD at €2,000 of cumulative deposits) only with compliance sign-off. Withdrawals always need VERIFIED plus a clear screening. | **Implemented 2026-10-29, off** (`KYC_BEFORE_DEPOSIT`, default `true`). A deposit is refused with `KYC_REQUIRED` until the KYC provider (Sumsub, or the built-in sandbox in preview) reports VERIFIED; with `false`, CDD starts at `KYC_CDD_THRESHOLD_MINOR` of lifetime deposits. A withdrawal needs VERIFIED and a CLEAR screening. |
| D18 | Real-money tables | A follow-up (Phase 2): deposits credit the wallet, tables keep playing chips, until rake, void-and-refund (D12) and certification are settled. | Tables use play chips only. The runtime's ledger types (`BUY_IN`, `CASH_OUT`, `RAKE`) exist and are unused. |
| D19 | Withdrawal cancellation | No reverse withdrawals: once requested, a withdrawal is paid, rejected by staff or fails at the provider. | **Implemented.** `ALLOW_WITHDRAWAL_CANCEL` defaults to `false` (`POST /api/wallet/withdrawals/:id/cancel` answers 403 `CANCEL_DISABLED`); if turned on, a player may cancel only while the withdrawal is under review. The database state machine has no way back from SUBMITTED: a SUBMITTED payout ends SETTLED or FAILED. Staff reject before dispatch; a "This wasn't me" lockdown or a chargeback cancels undispatched withdrawals. |
| D20 | Real-money switches | Three independent switches, all off by default: `PAYMENTS_MODE` (`off`/`sandbox`/`live`), `Asset.enabled` for EUR (database administrator), and one `PaymentSwitch` row per flow (two admins to turn on in live mode). | **Implemented 2026-10-29, all off.** Production `PAYMENTS_MODE=off`; the `poker-payments` Worker is not deployed, and poker-api treats its absent `PAYMENTS` binding as off (the wallet shows play chips only; every money route answers 403 `REAL_MONEY_DISABLED`). `EUR` disabled; every `PaymentSwitch` row seeded `false`. Preview runs `sandbox` (`TEST_EUR`, built-in provider) once the operator deploys it. Enabling: [PAYMENTS_RUNBOOK.md](../money/PAYMENTS_RUNBOOK.md). |
| D21 | Payments topology | A separate Worker, `poker-payments`, holds the payments database login, its own Hyperdrive config and every provider secret. `poker-api` forwards signed-in players over a typed RPC binding and never touches money itself. | **Implemented 2026-10-29, not deployed.** `apps/edge/wrangler.payments.jsonc` (deployed by the operator only; CI dry-runs it); poker-api's `PAYMENTS` binding is optional. Payments re-checks the session, the role and every money rule itself, and posts only through `ledger_post_payments`. Provider webhooks reach its own `workers.dev` host. |
| D22 | Staff approval of money | Every withdrawal is reviewed in Phase 1. In live mode two different administrators approve a manual withdrawal, turn a switch on and resolve an uncertain payout. Every admin decision needs a passkey step-up for exactly that decision; nobody approves their own withdrawal. | **Implemented.** The `LOW_PLAY` rule reviews every withdrawal until real-money tables exist (D18); `DUAL_APPROVAL_MIN_MINOR` (EUR 5,000) and high-risk or shared-destination withdrawals need two approvals in any mode. Turning a switch off or freezing an account is one admin, always, even during that admin's own security hold. Cloudflare Access is required for payments administration in live mode. |
| D23 | Refund destinations | A refund returns money to where it came from. A card deposit's refund goes to that card only. A crypto deposit's source address is not known, so an administrator names one of the player's own cooled-off payout addresses, and that refund is treated as a withdrawal. | **Implemented 2026-11-03, off.** A card refund is bound to its deposit's card and goes back even if the card was revoked. A crypto refund to a named address is refused while the account is frozen, on a security hold or not KYC and screening clear; a lockdown, a freeze or a revoked address stops it before it is sent. Money is sent only to an active destination (the source card of a card refund excepted); the database refuses to submit anything else. |
| D24 | Screening and an administrator's own account | A sanctions or PEP match is decided by a person through an audited route, never by a database edit. Staff never loosen their own restrictions. | **Implemented 2026-11-03, off.** `POST /api/admin/users/:userId/screening`: a match is one admin and stops unsent money (a confirmed match also freezes); `CLEAR` is two admins after the last screening change and does not lift a freeze. Deposits are refused while a match is on record. In live mode the Sumsub rail stays closed unless its level screens (`SUMSUB_SCREENING_INCLUDED=true`). On their own account an administrator can only freeze, and never decides their own screening. |

## Rake

The engine accepts `rakeBps` and `rakeCapChips`. The default is 0, and nothing in table creation sets a rake. Charging commission is an owner decision and is not recorded as taken. Zero rake must not be described as a commission system.

## Real money

No decision here authorizes deposits, custody, or redeemable chips. F01–F05 in the [29 September audit](../reviews/2026-09-29-backend-security-audit.md) are the prerequisites. The [ledger](../money/LEDGER.md) is unused by gameplay. The wallet (D16–D24: the `poker-payments` Worker, the wallet and admin routes, the database layer) exists with every switch off; see [WALLET.md](../money/WALLET.md).
